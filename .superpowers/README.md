# Maintained implementation planning

This entry owns navigation to reviewed implementation plans, their scope and
dated steps. [Current documentation](../docs/README.md) owns supported behavior,
architecture and evidence; a plan's task list alone does not establish delivery
or native acceptance.

- [plans/](plans/README.md) inventories the individually tracked implementation plans.

Before adding, replacing or relocating maintained planning, read the
[repository organization policy](../docs/policies/repository-organization.md).
Track a future reviewed plan through an exact per-file exception in root
`.gitignore` and a matching entry in `plans/README.md` in the same change.
Inspect exact destination collisions; do not broaden tracking or force-add private
work. Instruction-reference or loading changes also require the existing
[progressive-disclosure procedure](../docs/policies/documentation.md#instruction-progressive-disclosure).

Dated plans record the decisions, versions, commands and completed steps at their
date. Follow an active authority when the contributor instructions explicitly
require it; retain historical facts when preparing approved follow-up work.
The policies remain the owners of contribution, verification and security rules.

Private `sdd/`, scratch plans, logs, native stages and other experiment evidence
remain ignored. They are not inputs to a maintained planning relocation or a
public submission. Use the organization policy's bounded retention rules when
preserving private evidence within an authorized task.
